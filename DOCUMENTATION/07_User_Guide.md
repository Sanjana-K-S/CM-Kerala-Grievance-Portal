

\# User Guide



\## 1. Citizen



\### Registration and Login

1\. Open the portal.

2\. Register using your name, email, mobile number, and password.

3\. Log in using your registered credentials.



\### Submit a Grievance

1\. Open the Submit Grievance page.

2\. Select the district and category.

3\. Enter the subject and complaint description.

4\. Optionally upload a supporting attachment.

5\. Review AI suggestions if available.

6\. Submit the grievance.

7\. Save the generated reference number.



\### Track a Grievance

1\. Open Track Grievance.

2\. Enter the reference number.

3\. View the current status and available updates.



\### Feedback

After resolution, submit feedback through the citizen dashboard.



\## 2. CMO Administrator



1\. Log in using administrator credentials.

2\. Open the administrator dashboard.

3\. View grievance statistics and submitted complaints.

4\. Review grievance details.

5\. Assign grievances to departments and officers.

6\. Monitor grievance progress.



\## 3. Department Officer



1\. Log in using officer credentials.

2\. Open the officer dashboard.

3\. View assigned grievances.

4\. Review complaint details.

5\. Update the grievance status and enter remarks.



\## 4. Contact Us



Visitors can use the Contact Us page to submit enquiries through the contact form.



\## 5. Important Notes



\- Keep grievance reference numbers safe.

\- Upload only permitted file formats.

\- AI suggestions should be reviewed before action.

\- Use test accounts and fictional data for demonstrations.

\- This application is a demonstration prototype, not an official government service.

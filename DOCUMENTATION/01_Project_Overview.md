

\# CM Kerala Grievance Redressal Portal



\## 1. Project Overview



The CM Kerala Grievance Redressal Portal is a web-based demonstration prototype designed to simplify the submission, tracking, management, and resolution of public grievances.



It provides a centralized platform connecting citizens, CMO administrators, and department officers.



\*\*Disclaimer:\*\* This is a demonstration prototype and is not an official Government of Kerala website.



\## 2. Objectives



\- Allow citizens to register and submit grievances online.

\- Generate a unique reference number for each grievance.

\- Enable citizens to track grievance status.

\- Allow CMO administrators to review and assign grievances.

\- Enable officers to investigate and update assigned grievances.

\- Maintain grievance status updates and history.

\- Collect citizen feedback after resolution.

\- Use AI to suggest grievance category, department, priority, and summary.



\## 3. User Roles



\### Citizen

\- Register and log in.

\- Submit grievances with optional attachments.

\- Track submitted grievances.

\- View grievance status and updates.

\- Submit feedback.



\### CMO Administrator

\- View submitted grievances.

\- Monitor dashboard statistics.

\- Assign grievances to departments and officers.

\- Review grievance progress.



\### Department Officer

\- View assigned grievances.

\- Review grievance details.

\- Update grievance status and remarks.

\## 5. Grievance Workflow



1\. Citizen registers and logs in.

2\. Citizen submits a grievance.

3\. System generates a unique reference number.

4\. CMO administrator reviews the grievance.

5\. Grievance is assigned to a department and officer.

6\. Officer investigates and updates its status.

7\. Resolution details are submitted for review.

8\. Citizen can view updates and provide feedback.



\## 6. Grievance Statuses



\- Submitted

\- Under Review

\- In Progress

\- Resolved

\- Reopened



\## 7. AI Assistance



The portal integrates Google Gemini API to analyse grievance descriptions and suggest:

\- Relevant department

\- Grievance category

\- Priority level

\- Short grievance summary



AI suggestions are advisory and require human review. They do not make official decisions.



\## 8. Disclaimer



This application is developed as a demonstration project for evaluation purposes. It does not represent an official government service.
